# Open Geospatial & Hydrological Data Sources for India

## 1. Documented Open Datasets

### A. Digital Elevation Models (DEM)
- **Dataset:** SRTM 30m Global Topobathymetry / NASADEM
- **Source:** NASA JPL / USGS EarthExplorer / OpenTopography
- **License:** Public Domain / Open Access
- **CRS:** EPSG:4326 (WGS84)
- **Resolution:** 1 arc-second (~30m cell size)

### B. Indian Dam Structural Specifications
- **Dataset:** National Register of Large Dams (NRLD) & Central Water Commission (CWC)
- **Source:** Central Water Commission, Ministry of Jal Shakti, Govt. of India
- **Reference URL:** [https://cwc.gov.in/](https://cwc.gov.in/)
- **License:** Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India
- **Key Attributes:** Crest Length, Dam Height, Full Reservoir Level (FRL), Design Spillway Outflow.

### C. Downstream Built Infrastructure
- **Dataset:** OpenStreetMap (OSM) India Vector Extracts
- **Source:** Geofabrik / OpenStreetMap Contributors
- **License:** Open Database License (ODbL)
- **Layers:** Building footprints, NH/SH Highway networks, river crossing bridges, schools, and hospitals.

### D. Satellite Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
- **Dataset:** Copernicus Sentinel-1 C-Band SAR GRD
- **Source:** European Space Agency (ESA) / Google Earth Engine
- **License:** Copernicus Open Access Policy
- **Usage:** Near-real-time flood inundation observation and IoU model validation.
